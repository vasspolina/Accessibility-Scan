In the end, real people with real disabilities test your site. Their verdict depends on how it feels to use, not how many boxes a machine ticks.

Our principles follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG), the international standard for accessible websites. We test against its four principles:

Perceivable
Everyone can access your information, even if they can’t use all their senses
Operable
All your website’s functions work for everyone, no exceptions
Understandable
Your content is clear and your navigation makes sense
Robust
Your site works on any device with any assistive technology, today, tomorrow, and for the foreseeable future
