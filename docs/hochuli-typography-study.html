Detail in typography, set in Jokker

Jost Hochuli, Detail in typography (Hyphen Press, 2008). The widget’s typesetting rules came from this book on 18 Oct 2026, measured on PP Telegraf. The next day the font changed to Jokker VF. Several of those rules were reasons about Telegraf’s glyphs, so this study measures Jokker and asks which reasons still hold. Each section gives the book’s rule and page, what Jokker can do, and what the widget does now. Nothing in the code has been changed.

The dash: the reason for the em dash is gone

Book: p.36–40, figs 41–53 · Where: lib/typography.ts smarten()

Hochuli’s own dash is the spaced en dash. We set English with a close em dash instead, for one reason written into CLAUDE.md: in Telegraf the hyphen was 0.47em and the en dash 0.57em, so a spaced en dash read as a hyphen.

In Jokker the en dash is half as wide again as the hyphen, so it no longer reads as one. The close em dash is now the wide one: at 27px it is 25px of rule touching both words.

What would follow

  • A decision for you: English could take the spaced en dash, like de, fr and es already do, and every language would set the dash one way. smarten() already does this for the other three, so it is one branch to remove. The strings that were swept to the close em dash would need sweeping back.

French spacing falls back to another font

Book: p.36, fig.41 · Where: typography.ts:196, :107

French sets a narrow space before ; ! ? and inside « ». smarten() does this with U+202F, the narrow no-break space, which is right. Jokker has no glyph for U+202F, so the browser takes that one character from a fallback font, at that font’s width. The space is there, but its width depends on the reader’s system, not on us.

as set now (U+202F)Pourquoi ? « Refuser »

What would follow

  • Use Jokker’s thin space (U+2009) and keep it from breaking: wrap the mark and its space in a nowrap span. Jokker has no word joiner (U+2060) either, so that route is closed.

Figures: lining in prose, proportional in tables

Book: p.41–44 · Where: styles/global.css

Hochuli wants non-lining figures in running text, so numbers sit in the line like lowercase, and tabular figures in tables, so columns align. Jokker has no non-lining figures, so prose stays lining, and that cannot be fixed in CSS. Jokker does have tabular figures, and the widget never asks for them: font-variant-numeric appears nowhere in the widget. Its default figures are proportional, a 1 being 0.306em and a 0 being 0.612em.

Capitals in the line

Book: p.39–40, fig.54; letterspacing p.23–31 · Where: .a11y-caps, ScanChat

Hochuli sets abbreviations in small capitals, or failing that in capitals a size smaller and spaced evenly, with no holes at O, C and G. Jokker has no small capitals, so the widget’s workaround is the right one: 25px at +0.06em inside 27px prose. That spacing was judged on Telegraf and has not been checked on Jokker.

Jokker’s case feature raises hyphens, brackets and dashes to capital height. The widget never turns it on, so ‘WCAG-AA’ or ‘(ARIA)’ has its punctuation hung at lowercase height.

What would follow

  • Turn on case for .a11y-caps.
  • Re-judge +0.06em on Jokker by eye at O, C and G, using the specimen above.

Letterspacing follows the size, on old numbers

Book: p.23–31, figs 25–35 · Where: 61 rules at +0.01em, 4 at 0, 4 at −0.005em, 4 at −0.01em

The rule holds: spacing follows the counter, tighter as the size grows, never tracking lowercase for effect. The values do not yet. They were measured on Telegraf, where the space between two n was 0.13em against a counter of 0.29em. Jokker is a different face, with an n of 0.634em, an o of 0.635em and an x-height of 0.534em, and nobody has looked at it at our five sizes.

Emphasis, and the italic Jokker does not have

Book: p.41–44

The rule stands as written in CLAUDE.md: emphasis is Medium, and questions are told from answers by the label. Jokker’s variable file has a slnt axis, which is a sloped roman, not an italic, so the rule does not change. The font-synthesis: none setting keeps the browser from faking one.

What already follows the book

  • Quotation marks by language, single first in English (fig.53), and a short quoted phrase’s comma set outside it.
  • Three dots become one ellipsis sign, which Jokker has.
  • One- and two-letter words bound to the next word with a no-break space, so they never end a line.
  • A 60ch measure, text-wrap: pretty and hyphenate-limit-chars: 7 3 3 for a soft rag (p.32–35).
  • List markers hung outside the text, en dash or figure.
  • Code as an inserted passage at 18px on the text’s line (p.47–53, figs 68, 70).

One knowing departure: Hochuli prefers a first-line indent to a line space between paragraphs. The chat uses half a line, on your instruction of 4 Nov 2026 after the Displaay FAQ. It stays.

In short

Three changes are small and plainly right: tabular figures in tables and stat tiles, case on runs of capitals, and a thin space that Jokker has instead of the narrow space it lacks. One is a decision: with Jokker’s narrow hyphen, English can return to Hochuli’s spaced en dash. One is work: re-measure the letterspacing on Jokker.
