How the layout is built

The game is replayed move by move. The pieces left on the board after the last move give the title its squares: characters fill them in reading order, a8 to h1. The same squares hold the info items on the smaller board at the bottom. Each background square is tinted by how many of the game’s positions it was occupied in, from the start position to the end. The destination of the last move is set in the accent colour.
