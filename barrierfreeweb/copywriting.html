Clear

Accessible
Easy to grasp for a broad audience
Scannable
Short paragraphs with logical breaks and clear bullet points
Inclusive
Respectful and resonant with diverse readers
Descriptive
Links and alt text that explain purpose, not just “click here”

Logical flow without visual cues like “see below”
